# GasSeva — Abyssal Obsidian & Metallic Champagne Luxury Design System (3D Edition)

## 1. Palette Specifications
- **Canvas Background (`ObsidianCanvas`)**: `#070C18` (Deep Abyssal Space)
- **Primary Container (`ObsidianContainer`)**: `#0E172A` (Rich Midnight Glass)
- **High Elevation Container (`ObsidianContainerHigh`)**: `#16233B` (Elevated Tactile Glass)
- **Primary Action (`ElectricCobalt`)**: `#3B82F6` (Electric Cobalt Blue)
- **Primary Accent (`ChampagneGold`)**: `#E2C384` (Metallic Gold Highlight)
- **Secondary Accent (`CobaltGlow`)**: `#60A5FA` (Neon Radiance)
- **Verification Green (`GasSevaGreen`)**: `#10B981` (Emerald Gemstone)
- **Verification Dark (`GasSevaGreenDark`)**: `#059669` (Deep Emerald Shadow)
- **Error Red (`StatusError`)**: `#F43F5E` (Ruby Crimson)
- **Text Primary (`TextPrimary`)**: `#F8FAFC` (Pure Ice White)
- **Text Secondary (`TextSecondary`)**: `#94A3B8` (Muted Metallic Silver)
- **Card Border (`CardBorder`)**: `#1E293B` with `#E2C384` (Metallic Gold Trims)

## 2. 3D Elevation & Material Depth
- **Glassmorphism**: Multi-layer radial gradients with subtle translucent overlays (`alpha = 0.85f`).
- **3D Checkmark Orb**: Multi-tiered 3D sphere with specular light highlight, 3D radial depth gradient, metallic gold rim, drop-shadow elevation, and 3D checkmark tick.
- **Tactile Touch Feedback**: Press elevation scale & glowing borders on interactive buttons.
- **Metallic Borders**: 1.5dp borders with gold/cobalt gradients for a rich, luxury look.
